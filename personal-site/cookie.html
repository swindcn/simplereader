Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how LucidRead (the "Application"), developed by WildGrassX Studio, uses — or more accurately, does not use — cookies and similar technologies.

1. Our Stance on Cookies

The Application is designed as an Accessibility-First, No-Login service. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or tracking pixels to profile you across the web.

2. What We Do NOT Use

Specifically, the Application contains:

  • No third-party advertising cookies — we serve no ads.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs — we do not measure or sell your behavior.
  • No cross-site tracking — we cannot follow you between websites or apps.

3. Local-Only Preferences

The only data stored on your device is your own reading content and a few app preferences (such as your theme or language choice). These are kept in your local device storage or your private Apple iCloud account, not in cookies, and are never transmitted to us.

4. Managing Cookies in iOS

Because the Application itself sets no tracking cookies, there is nothing for you to clear inside the app. If you open our website from the app, you may manage cookies through iOS Safari settings (Settings → Apps → Safari → Advanced → Website Data).

5. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us at: support@wildgrassx.com.
