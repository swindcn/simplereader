Arrow Dash Saga Data Choices and Deletion

WildGrassX Studio · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Local progress and settings

Settings → Reset all progress resets the current and unlocked levels. It preserves music volume, sound and haptic preferences and does not erase energy records, ad-provider records or purchases.

To remove the app's local data, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Arrow Dash Saga → Delete App. Read the iOS confirmation carefully: local saves may be lost. Offload App is different and can retain documents and data. iOS backups may still contain an earlier copy; manage relevant backups through your Apple settings. We cannot remotely inspect, export or delete local-only saves on your device. You do not need to contact us or submit identification to reset local progress.

Advertising choices and provider-held records

When available, open Settings → Advertising privacy options in the game to revisit the choices presented by Google's UMP consent service. Options depend on region and provider configuration. A change can affect subsequent ad availability; it is not a deletion request for past records.

For Google's independently processed advertising information, review Google's Privacy Policy and its available privacy controls. We cannot directly delete records independently controlled by Google. Contact us if you need help identifying the relevant service or exercising a right relating to information we hold.

Purchases

Deleting the app or resetting progress does not cancel, erase or refund an Apple transaction. Restore access with the original Apple Account through Settings → Restore purchases. Apple handles its purchase records and refund requests; see Apple Privacy and refund help. A refunded or revoked purchase may lose its entitlement.

Requests to WildGrassX Studio

Email support@wildgrassx.com with the subject “Arrow Dash Saga privacy request.” Describe whether you want access, correction, deletion or another applicable right and which information is involved. The current game has no developer-operated account to delete. We can address information we actually hold, such as support correspondence, and explain provider or device steps for other data.

We may ask only for proportionate information necessary to locate the records and verify the request. Do not send passwords, full card details or identity documents unless a specific, lawful need has been explained through an appropriate process. Requests are handled within applicable legal time limits and may be subject to lawful retention exceptions. Where permitted and necessary, authorized agents can contact us; reasonable authorization checks may apply.

See the Privacy Policy for purposes, retention, recipients and other rights. You may contact a competent data protection authority where available under law.
