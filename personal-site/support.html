LucidRead Support & Accessibility Guide

Welcome to LucidRead. We are dedicated to providing a clean, ad-free, and accessibility-first reading experience.

VoiceOver Gesture Shortcuts

  • Play / Pause: Two-finger double tap (Magic Tap) anywhere on the screen.
  • Next / Previous Chapter: Three-finger swipe left or right.
  • Adjust Speech Rate: Three-finger swipe up or down.
  • Exit Reader / Save Progress: Two-finger Z-shape scrub.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I import my books?
A: You can open any TXT or EPUB file in the iOS Files app, Safari, or WeChat, tap "Share", and select LucidRead to import instantly.

Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?
A: Simply open the Paywall or Settings page in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". No login required.
